What's in a Name

"Desmosomes are specialized intercellular junctions that act as molecular 'spot welds' to tightly bind adjacent cells together. Highly concentrated in tissues that endure significant mechanical stress, such as the skin, heart, and bladder, they maintain tissue structural integrity by resisting tearing and physical shear forces."
— on desmosomes, cell biology

desmoVet is built on the same principle. No single tool holds up veterinary practice or education on its own — but placed where the stress actually is, and connected to everything around them, they're built to take the load: fast decisions, incomplete information, and the students and clinicians who need something that holds up under pressure.
