Intervals below are from published neonatology texts (see Sources) and are not a substitute for your own laboratory's validated intervals.

On small, skewed neonatal samples this arithmetic can push a lower bound below zero, so treat flags near the low boundary of any derived interval as a prompt to check the value rather than as a result in itself.
