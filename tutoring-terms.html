Terms of Service

Version 1.0 · Effective August 1, 2026

1.Who we are

desmoVet Peer Tutoring is a peer tutoring service operated by KB Enterprises LLC. We match veterinary students with tutors who have already studied the same material.

We are independent. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by any veterinary college. Your school's policies and honor code apply to you in full and take priority over anything here.

By booking a session you agree to these terms and to the Academic Integrity Policy, which is part of this agreement.

2.What we do and don't do

We do: match you with a tutor, handle scheduling and payment, and set standards for how sessions run.

We don't: employ your tutor. Tutors are independent contractors who set their own availability and decide how to teach. We vet them, we hold them to standards, and we remove them when they don't meet those standards — but we don't control the content of any individual session.

We don't promise results. No tutor and no service can guarantee you a grade, a pass, or a class rank. Anyone who tells you otherwise is being dishonest with you. What we offer is a faster path to understanding the material.

We don't claim to know your curriculum. The program has been restructured and no tutor has completed it in its new form. Tutors are prohibited from implying otherwise or speculating about assessment content.

3.Booking and payment

  • One-on-one tutoring is $45.00 per hour, billed in 30-minute increments after the first hour
  • Group sessions are $15.00 per person for a one-hour session
  • Payment is due before the session. We'll send a payment link once you're matched; the session is confirmed when payment clears
  • Prepaid packages, when offered, are used within the semester purchased and are non-transferable to another person
  • A limited number of reduced-rate places are available each semester for students who need them. Email tutoring@desmovet.com — we don't ask for documentation, and we don't tell your tutor

Group sessions

  • Group sessions hold four to six students and cover one topic
  • A session runs only if at least four seats are filled. If it doesn't reach four, we'll offer you a place in the next session or refund your seat in full — you are never charged for a session that didn't run
  • Seats are prepaid and are not refundable within 24 hours of the session, because it runs whether or not you attend and your tutor is paid either way
  • Seats are transferable — if you can't make it, tell us who's taking your place

4.Cancellations and no-shows

  • Cancel 24 or more hours ahead: no charge, full refund or credit
  • Cancel with less than 24 hours' notice, or don't show: you're charged 50% of the session. Your tutor receives all of it — they set aside the time
  • Arriving late: your tutor waits 15 minutes, then the session counts as a no-show
  • If your tutor cancels or doesn't show: you're not charged, and we'll rematch or rebook you at no cost

Life in vet school is unpredictable and emergencies happen. Tell us — one-off waivers are at our discretion and we're not unreasonable about them.

5.Rematching and refunds

Not the right tutor? We'll rematch you free.

Tutoring is personal and not every pairing works. Tell us and we'll move you to a different tutor at no charge. You don't need to explain, and we won't tell your previous tutor why.

Completed sessions aren't refundable. Your tutor prepared for and delivered the session, and is paid for it — the same as any other professional service. What we can do is make sure the next one is with someone who suits you better.

Where something actually went wrong — a session cut short, a tutor who didn't turn up or was clearly unprepared, a charge that looks incorrect — email tutoring@desmovet.com within 7 days and we'll put it right. That's a genuine service failure, not a preference, and it's treated differently.

Unused prepaid package sessions are refundable on request, minus any sessions already used at the standard rate. Group seats are covered in section 3.

6.Academic integrity

Your tutor cannot help with graded work, discuss exam content, or share school-owned materials. The full rules are in the Academic Integrity Policy and you should read it before your first session.

If you ask a tutor to break those rules, your access to the service ends. We won't report you to your school — we're not an arm of your institution — but we will stop working with you. The only exception is if we're legally required to disclose something, or if we're ourselves the subject of an institutional inquiry, in which case we'll be honest about our own records.

7.Conduct

Treat your tutor as a professional colleague. They're a student too, usually a year or more ahead of you and under the same pressure.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, or abusive behaviour toward a tutor or toward us ends your access immediately with no refund. This has never been a problem and we expect it never will be, but it needs to be written down.

8.Your information and how we use it

What we collect: your name, email, school year, the courses and topics you need help with, your session history, and your feedback.

What your tutor sees: what you write in your intake form and anything you tell them.

What we do with session notes. After each session your tutor writes a short note about what you worked on and where the sticking points were. We use these two ways:

  • To match you better next time and to improve how we train tutors
  • In aggregate and anonymised, to identify topics that many students find difficult — which informs group review sessions and free public study resources we publish

To be concrete: "students frequently confuse these two structures" may become a public study guide. Your name, your specific struggles, and anything identifying you never will. If you'd rather your sessions be excluded from that aggregate entirely, email tutoring@desmovet.com and we'll flag your account. It costs you nothing and changes nothing else about your service.

Confidentiality. We don't discuss your performance or your use of this service with classmates, faculty, or administrators. Tutors sign a confidentiality obligation covering the same. We may disclose information where legally required.

We don't sell your data, ever, to anyone.

Recording. No session is recorded without your written consent and your tutor's, given for that specific session. You can always say no.

Deletion. Ask us to delete your personal information at tutoring@desmovet.com and we will, apart from records we need to keep for tax and accounting purposes.

9.Liability

We provide tutoring services in good faith. To the extent the law allows, our total liability to you is limited to the amount you've paid us in the previous three months.

We're not responsible for academic outcomes, for decisions you make based on a session, or for the conduct of a tutor outside the scope of their agreement with us.

Nothing here limits liability that can't legally be limited.

10.Changes and contact

We may update these terms. Material changes get a new version number and effective date here, and anyone with an active booking gets an email. Continuing to book after a change means you accept it.

Governing law: Colorado.

Questions, problems, complaints: tutoring@desmovet.com. We answer within one business day.
